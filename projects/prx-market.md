## Project Summary

PRX Market is a fully functional decentralized prediction market platform currently live on **Sui Testnet** with **$9.88M+ in trading volume** and **$294K TVL**. We propose to port this battle-tested platform to Gno.land, bringing real user engagement and a proven product-market fit to the Gno ecosystem.

Our platform enables users to predict short-term BTC price movements (UP/DOWN) in 30-minute rounds, with fully on-chain settlement and real-time oracle integration. The system includes a USDC staking pool that provides yield to liquidity providers while maintaining platform solvency.
